# Brief

## Question

Under what financing and governance conditions can a building carry a credible provision for its own recurring renewal, and what could a commercial, replaceable worldwide steward contribute?

## Claim and serious alternative

Renewal depends on capital genuinely committed to service, the timing and covariance of income and expenditure, and institutions able to perform and revise work. A dedicated endowment can support that commitment but does not remove its opportunity cost or outperform a comparably funded conventional reserve by definition. The strongest rival is an ordinary well-capitalized reserve governed competently. Give it equal resources and operating authority in the model.

## Study commitment, 2026-10-10

Derive launch feasibility for the 125-apartment example. Model 60 years of recurring component work and a rolling 20-year planning horizon. Compare conventional reserves, a rental endowment and a financial endowment with equal initial dedicated economic capital and explicit later contributions. Track delayed work and terminal continuation as well as cash. Stress investment loss, vacancy, component failure, cost escalation, fees and decision delay. Assumptions are examples, not estimated market forecasts. Omit the seed's unreproduced historical S&P/FX return figure.
