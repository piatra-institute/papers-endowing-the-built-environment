# Editorial record

Reviewed 2026-10-10. Full manuscript and all rendered pages read. This is an
agent editorial review, not external peer review or local professional advice.

## Contribution and reader

A financing argument and an institutional proposal for recurring building
renewal. The model tests launch resources, cash timing, performed work and
continuation provision. The substantial second half develops what a replaceable
commercial steward could do, without treating the model as evidence that this
company already exists or that a universal legal wrapper is available.

## Material worth preserving

The handover opening makes the obligation concrete. Foregone selling value,
the integer launch constraint and equal initial dedicated capital prevent the
retained apartments from becoming free capital in the argument. The matched
reserve is a serious control. The distinction between a continuing purpose and
an irremovable provider gives the institutional half a specific problem to solve.

## Reverse outline

- After the keys change hands: identifies the continuing obligation and the ordinary-reserve rival.
- What must be maintained: distinguishes service, asset value, cash provision and lifecycle evaluation.
- The capital has to come from somewhere: reconciles the development account and derives launch bounds.
- A building through several renewal cycles: defines recurrence, timing, arrears and the rolling funding test.
- What the funding comparisons show: reports baseline, equivalence, extra capital and stress results without a perpetual-solvency promise.
- The steward's mandate: converts a projection into duties and decisions.
- Authority without an irremovable manager: separates ownership, engineering, authorization, custody and service delivery.
- What can be shared across borders: treats common expertise, pooling, currency and bounded legal differences.
- Paying for continuity: prices the manager and distinguishes client protection from company growth and affordability.
- Failure, adaptation and exit: specifies replacement and purpose revision rather than asserting perpetuity.
- Model record: states finite-horizon and no-hidden-rescue limits.

## Strongest rival

An adequately funded, competently managed reserve holding the same investments
produces exactly the same ledger as the financial endowment. The manuscript
states this in the abstract and results. Any advantage must therefore concern
the durability and performance of the commitment, not the word “endowment”.
That institutional advantage is proposed for testing, not demonstrated by the
simulation.

failure procedures, not a short speculative conclusion. Concrete accounts and
service decisions lead the prose. The ending leaves residents usable records,
funded obligations and replaceable management, rather than a slogan about perpetuity.
