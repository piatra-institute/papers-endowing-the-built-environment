# Research and inference record

## Primary evidence inspected, 2026-10-10

- T1 NIST HB 135e2025, chapter 3, especially sections 3.3.1-3.3.3: consistent real/nominal accounting, relative price escalation, and the distinction between present-value evaluation and appropriated budgets. Its example prices are not current local cost estimates.
- T1 Merton WP3820, author abstract at NBER: endowment policy depends on other institutional resources and uncertain activity costs. The use here is this framing, not a reproduction of his optimization theorem.
- T1 Ostrom's revised Nobel lecture draft, introduction and section 2A, pp. 1-4, Indiana University's archive: different service activities can operate at different scales, with independent local providers and shared larger services. Paraphrase the draft as such; do not claim the proposed company has empirical validation from these studies.
- T1 Land Trust official Endowment page and Parks Trust Our Story: close operational precedents. The latter reports a property endowment at establishment and continuing investment/property income plus other support. Neither organization's promotional claim of perpetual provision establishes a guarantee for housing.
- T1 Romania, Law 196/2018, articles 66-71: separate client records; management transfer; common-property income dedicated to repairs; annual repair-fund contributions. A separately titled rental apartment is not automatically common property.
- T1 NYC HPD Local Law 97 guidance: category-dependent emissions/compliance obligations illustrate adaptation liabilities beyond like-for-like replacement. This source does not create a universal condominium reserve requirement.
- T1 Maharashtra Act section 70, India Code PDF p. 47: specified investment/deposit routes and rule/order permissions. No assertion that a housing society may freely buy the model's equity portfolio. The official 2025 consolidation and later rule notices are listed by the state; deployment needs the applicable housing provisions and current orders checked by counsel.
- T1 Shanghai Housing Administration response 2024/9, sections I-III: procedures, audits, government-bond/deposit routes and then-current use of term deposits. Cite as a dated official account, not as a complete 2026 legal opinion. City special-fund regulations also distinguish ownership, use and manager changes.
- T1 Kenya Law, current English endpoint resolves to 2022-12-31 consolidation: sections 20, 21 and 35 respectively address common-property duties and funding, no trading powers, and trustee-permitted investments with special resolution. No universal permission for the condominium corporation to operate the rental endowment.

## Model and conceptual decisions

All values in config.json are openly stated illustrative assumptions. No five-city underwriting, historical return backtest, default probability or perpetual guarantee is claimed. The launch calculation prices apartments at foregone sale value. The conventional reserve receives the same initial capital; a matched-portfolio control returns identical numbers.

The rolling forecast reports a funding gap but does not automatically compel contributions: authority and collection are institutional questions. Negative operating balances denote unpaid obligations, not an available zero-interest loan or continued safe service. Component age beyond its schedule denotes overdue work, not an empirically estimated failure probability. End-period property value is not added to future rental income when testing liquid continuation funding.

## Argument risks

The simulations cannot establish that legal commitment improves compliance. They show what funding and timing require under assumptions. The institutional proposal must therefore stand on explicit authority, conflicts controls and replaceability; its costs and legal compatibility remain matters for a pilot and qualified local review.

Unverified seed claims about tax treatment, universal investment powers, historical compounding and novelty are excluded. Financial continuation, resident affordability and ecological benefit require different evidence and different governance choices.
